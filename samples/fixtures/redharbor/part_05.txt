Friday the fog came down as promised. Briggs filed his report. The hold was
empty of anything worth a customs stamp, and the *Moth* took the tide out with
Cael at the rail.

Ella — who was, in fact, still in Lythe, according to the note her sister sent
that noon — did not answer when Briggs knocked at the pilot's house. The house
was dark. The cracked compass pieces were in a saucer on the kitchen shelf,
where she had left them Wednesday night.

Briggs stood in the doorway with the deferral letter in his hand and read the
strange signature again.

"Who signed this?" he said to the empty room. "Who told Whitby they could come
a day early, and on whose authority?"

Nobody answered him. The *Moth* cleared the bar and was gone.
