INT. HALBERD STREET — GROUND FLOOR LOBBY — DAY

Two ENGINEERS have the lift doors open and the chain off. One is inside the
shaft. The brass plate has been unscrewed and lies on the floor with the marker
writing face down.

MRS PARIS
Eleven months.

ENGINEER
It's the governor. It was always the governor.

He closes the doors. The lift hums, and goes up, and comes down, and the
indicator needle swings through the floors for the first time since the fire.

MRS PARIS
(to nobody)
Now they come.

INT. HALBERD STREET — MARA'S FLAT — NIGHT

Mara opens the door before Dev knocks. He is standing on the landing with his
hands where she can see them.

MARA
You've been in that car since the sixteenth.

DEV
Yes.

MARA
Mrs Paris says you're police.

DEV
Mrs Paris is right.

Mara stands aside. Dev comes in. She takes the lid off the cistern, lifts out
the tobacco tin, opens it, and puts the cassette flat on his palm.

MARA
That's the only one. There was never a copy. If you lose it I've got nothing and
neither have you.

DEV
I know what it is.

MARA
You don't yet.

He closes his hand on it. She watches him do it.
