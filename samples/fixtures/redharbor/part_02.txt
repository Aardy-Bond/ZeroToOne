On Wednesday the *Moth*'s mate, CAEL ORR, came up to the pilot's house with a
cut on his hand and a story about a spar that had swung. Ella cleaned it. He saw
the compass on its cord when her coat fell open.

"That's a good one," Cael said.

"It was my brother's. It doesn't leave this coat."

That evening a storm rolled in from the north and the *Moth* slammed against the
quay piles hard enough to throw crockery. Ella ran down. In the scramble on the
wet deck someone knocked the compass from her hand; it hit the stone and the
brass body cracked clean through. The needle jammed. She picked up the pieces
and put them in her pocket, and for the first time in nine years she had no
bearing but the harbour lights.

Briggs came out with a lantern. The padlock on the *Moth*'s hold was still
closed. He checked it twice, as if the storm might have opinions about customs.
