Thursday morning Ella left Redharbor before light. Her sister in Lythe had sent
word that their mother had taken a turn, and Ella walked the coast path with a
borrowed coat and no compass, and she did not mean to be back before Saturday.

She told Briggs at the slip. "You're in charge of the board until I'm back. If
the Whitby man comes early, you meet him. I won't be on the quay."

"Go," Briggs said.

Cael Orr watched her climb the path until she was a dark mark against the chalk,
then went aboard the *Moth* and sat on the hatch of the sealed hold as if it
were a bench. The padlock was cold under his palm.

In the pilot's house, empty now, a letter lay on the table that Ella had not
opened: a deferral of the customs inspection, signed in a hand neither Briggs
nor Cael recognised. Who signed it, and with what authority, nobody on the quay
could say.
