INT. HALBERD STREET — GROUND FLOOR LOBBY — MORNING

A brass plate beside the lift reads OUT OF SERVICE. Somebody has written UNTIL
WHEN under it in marker. The lift doors are chained.

MRS PARIS, 70s, the caretaker, is mopping around the chain.

MRS PARIS
Since the fire. Eleven months. They send a man to look at it and the man looks
at it and goes away again.

Mara comes down the last of the stairs with her coat over her arm.

MARA
Is there a list? For the man to look at?

MRS PARIS
There's a list. You're not on it.

Mara goes out. Mrs Paris watches her go.

EXT. HALBERD STREET — MARKET — DAY

Mara buys nothing and looks at everything. Dev falls in beside her at a stall of
secondhand radio parts, a stranger with a valve in his hand.

DEV
That one's dead.

MARA
They're all dead. That's why they're on the table.

DEV
Dev.

MARA
(not giving hers)
Mm.

She moves off. Dev does not follow. He turns the valve over twice and puts it
down.

DEV
(quietly, to nobody)
Three weeks and she hasn't looked at me once.

INT. HALBERD STREET — MARA'S FLAT — NIGHT

Mara lifts the cistern lid, takes out the tin, checks the cassette is in it,
puts it back. She does this the way other people lock a door.
