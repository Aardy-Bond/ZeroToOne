Thursday night the Whitby customs man came early after all. Before anyone
touched the padlock, Cael Orr told him — as a man reports what he has already
seen — that the beef crates inside the still-sealed hold were stacked three deep
against the starboard ribs and that the second crate from the back had a broken
strap. The hold had not been opened since Briggs locked it.

Briggs then met the customs man at the slip with the key, unlocked the *Moth*'s
hold in front of him, and stood aside. What they found was ordinary: rope, tar,
crates of tinned beef. Briggs looked almost disappointed.

On the quay, in the lantern light, Ella Venn took a bearing with her brother's
brass compass — glass whole, needle swinging free — and pointed the customs man
toward the path up to the coast road.

"You'll want to leave before the fog thickens," she said. She had left Redharbor
before light that morning for Lythe and told Briggs she would not be back before
Saturday. Yet here she was on the quay on Thursday night, compass in hand.
