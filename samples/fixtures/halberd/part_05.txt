INT. HALBERD STREET — LIFT — DAY

Mara and Dev ride up together. The lift is slow and loud and neither of them
says anything until the second floor.

MARA
I knew you were police the first night you came up those stairs. I knew before
you'd got to the second landing.

DEV
You didn't know me from the wall.

MARA
I knew you from the wall from the start. I let you sit in that car for three
weeks because I wanted to see who'd come and tap on your window.

The lift stops at the third floor.

INT. HALBERD STREET — MARA'S FLAT — CONTINUOUS

The door is open. It should not be.

Dev goes in first. The cistern lid is on the floor in two pieces. The tobacco tin
is on the bed, open and empty, and the room has been gone through in the
unhurried way of somebody who had a long time and no reason to hurry.

DEV
It's in my coat. It's been in my coat since Tuesday.

MARA
Then they don't know that.

She goes to the window. Down in the street, Mrs Paris is putting a bucket into
the boot of a car that is not hers and getting into the passenger seat.

MARA (CONT'D)
Eleven months that lift was out. Eleven months nobody could come up those stairs
without her hearing it.

The car pulls away. Neither of them moves.

MARA (CONT'D)
Who called it in, Dev? Three weeks ago. Who called it in?
